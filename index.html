Beacon — a light board

A light board for CVI

Black field. One light. He does not have to reach it.

There is nothing on this screen to aim at and nothing to press. The light answers a sound he makes, a movement he makes, a switch, or contact anywhere at all on the glass. He acts, the light grows, the kalimba answers. That loop is the whole game, and it does not ask for hands, legs, or words.

What the light does

Where it sits

Most children see better in one part of their field than the rest. Put the light where he turns to look, and in Reach mode put it where his hand can actually get to.

Color

Red and yellow are usually seen first. Change it if one holds him longer.

Waiting for him

How long it waits

Used by Call and answer. The silence is doing the work, so resist shortening it. Eight seconds feels long to you and is often about right for him.

This session

Counted on this device only, and cleared when you reload. Latency is the gap between the board asking and him answering, which is the number his therapists will care about most.

Why it looks this plain

With cortical visual impairment the eyes usually work; the brain struggles to sort what they send. Clutter, patterned backgrounds, and more than one thing at a time all cost him the image. So this board shows a single saturated target on pure black, moves it slowly, and stops often.

Hold a session to a few minutes. Looking is tiring. If he stops tracking, that is the end of the session, not a reason to make the light bigger.
